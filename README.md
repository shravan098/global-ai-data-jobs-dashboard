# Global AI & Data Jobs Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing global AI and Data job market trends from 2020 to 2026.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes 90,000 AI and Data-related job postings across 12 countries.

The dashboard explores:

- Hiring trends over time
- Average salaries by job role
- Salary comparison across countries
- Experience-level distribution
- Work mode distribution
- AI specialization
- AI adoption
- Automation risk
- Job security
- Career growth
- Employee satisfaction

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains 90,000 records and 35 columns.

### Main Features

- Country
- Job Role
- AI Specialization
- Experience Level
- Experience Years
- Salary
- Bonus
- Education Required
- Industry
- Company Size
- Interview Rounds
- Year
- Work Mode
- AI Adoption Score
- Automation Risk
- Job Security Score
- Career Growth Score
- Work Life Balance Score
- Employee Satisfaction

## 📈 Dashboard Sections

### 1. Overview
Provides a high-level view of:

- Total job postings
- Average salary
- AI adoption
- Career growth
- Remote jobs
- Employee satisfaction
- Job security
- Hiring trends

### 2. Role & Skills
Analyzes job roles, salaries, experience levels and AI specializations.

### 3. Global Analytics
Compares countries based on:

- Average salary
- Hiring volume
- Layoff risk
- Work mode
- Industry
- Experience level
